    06 / FAQ
    
                
    Straight answers.
    
            
    
            
    
                
    
                    How does it work?+
                    
    A small helper on each cluster or server reports every 30 seconds. It only watches.
    
                
    
                
    
                    Which Linux servers?+
                    
    Any systemd distro on x86_64 or arm64: Ubuntu, Debian, RHEL, Rocky, Amazon Linux, Fedora. NVIDIA and AMD GPUs included.
    
                
    
                
    
                    Can I watch several at once?+
                    
    Yes, as many as you run, side by side.
    
                
    
                
    
                    Will it change anything?+
                    
    Only after you approve. Every change is logged and can be undone.
    
                
    
                
    
                    What about my passwords and keys?+
                    
    We show where a leaked one is, masked. The secret never leaves your machines.
