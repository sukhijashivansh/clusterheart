01 / Core

Everything you manage, locally

Live views of pods, jobs, and nodes, with one-click actions on every row.

Pods, Live

  • • Real-time health: ready count, status, restarts, and live CPU/memory from metrics-server.
  • • Find problems fast: filter by namespace, sort by CPU/memory/restarts, or flip on "issues only".
  • • Act in one click: logs, exec, port-forward, YAML, delete, and auto-diagnose.

Jobs

  • • Full picture: every job, including completed and lingering ones, with status and completion detail.
  • • Spot failures: see which jobs errored or never finished, right next to their pods.
  • • Drill down: jump from a job to its pod's logs, YAML, or diagnosis in one click.

Nodes

  • • Capacity vs. usage: allocatable CPU, memory, and ephemeral storage against live usage, per node.
  • • Health and role: Ready status and node role (control plane or worker).
  • • Packing view: pods-per-node against the pod limit, so you see crowding and headroom.

03 / AI Copilot

Ask your cluster anything

  • Full cluster context

    Understands your workloads, metrics, events, and cost. No copy-pasting YAML into a chatbot.

  • Answers from live data

    Runs read-only kubectl (get, describe, logs, top) so answers come from your cluster, not guesses.

  • Read-only, bring your own key

    Every mutating command is refused. Plug in Gemini or OpenRouter. Free models work out of the box.

04 / Rightsizing

Recommend & apply

  • Based on real usage, not guesses

    Samples CPU/memory every 30s over a 6h window and sizes to p95 + peak, so spiky apps never get starved.

  • Clear verdicts

    OK / UPSIZE CPU / UPSIZE MEM / DOWNSIZE, each with a concrete suggested request.

  • One-click apply

    Patch the new request straight to the owning workload. No YAML editing.
