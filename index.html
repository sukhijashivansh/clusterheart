01 / Core

Everything you manage, locally

Live views of the workloads that matter, with one-click actions on every row.

Pods, live

  • • Live health at a glance: ready count, status, restarts, and real-time CPU/memory from metrics-server.
  • • Find problems fast: namespace filter, search, sort by CPU/memory/restarts, and an "issues only" toggle.
  • • Act in one click: logs, YAML, shell, port-forward, delete, and one-click diagnose.

Jobs

  • • Full picture: every job including completed/lingering ones, with status and completion detail.
  • • Spot failures: see which jobs errored or never finished, right next to their pods.
  • • Drill down instantly: jump to a job's pod logs, YAML, or diagnosis without leaving the app.

Nodes

  • • Capacity vs. usage: allocatable CPU, memory, and ephemeral storage against live usage per node.
  • • Health and role: Ready status and node role (control plane or worker) at a glance.
  • • Packing view: pods-per-node vs. the pod limit, so you see crowding and headroom.

03 / AI Copilot

Ask questions from AI

  • Cluster-aware copilot

    Chat that understands your workloads, metrics, events, and cost context.

  • Reads your live cluster

    Runs allowlisted, read-only kubectl (get / describe / logs / top / events…) to answer with real data.

  • Safe & flexible

    Every mutating command is refused. Bring your own key: Gemini or OpenRouter, free models work out of the box.

04 / Rightsizing

Recommend & apply

  • History, not a guess

    Samples CPU/memory every 30s over a full window (6h default), sizing to p95 + peak so spiky apps aren't shrunk.

  • Clear verdicts

    OK / UPSIZE CPU / UPSIZE MEM / DOWNSIZE, each with a concrete suggested request.

  • One-click apply

    Patch the new resource request straight to the owning workload, right from the UI.

Most clusters quietly overpay. Pods ask for far more CPU and memory than they actually use, so AWS keeps bigger nodes than you need. Clusterheart shows you that waste in plain language, tells you exactly what you can reclaim, and patches the requests so your cluster can safely move down to a smaller, cheaper instance.
